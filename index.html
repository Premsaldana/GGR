Accommodations

5 bedroom Villa with private pool

The 5 bedroom villa is in a secured gated compound. The villa has a private swimming pool only for the exclusive use of guests. The guests can have food at the pool side dining area. A beautiful garden with lawn & flowers surrounds the villa.
The Villa has five 1 bedroom apartments with separate entrance door. Each apartment has a living room, a bedroom, and bathroom. A balcony with sun beds and sitting area.

The villa can accomodate maximun 20 guests

4 bedroom Villa


It has four luxury 1 bedroom apartments each sleeping a maximun of 4 people. It has common swimming pool. It can accomodate maximum 16 guests
All our suites sleep four adults. They are tastefully designed and all have two air-conditioning units, one bedroom and one lounge with additional overhead fans. There is Broadbandconnection to all suites and a business workstation in the reception.

1 bedroom Villa

This semi-detached private villa has all the facilities as the apartments. Its compact private rear garden and the extensive, private roof garden provide plenty of sun, shade and seclusion where guests can dine and while away the evenings with a bottle of fine wine.
It can accomodate maximun of 4 guests.

The villa is fully furnished with a double sofa bed in the living room and king side double bed in the bedroom. An additional single mattress can be made up. The safe is in the wardrobe. The modern, western style bathroom is complete with bathtub – shower and dual electrical sockets.
